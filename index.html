HOW TO PLAY

MOVEMENT

WASD / Arrow Keys (desktop) or the virtual joystick (mobile) to move your ship in any direction.

SHOOTING

SPACE or Left Mouse (desktop) or the FIRE button (mobile). Hold to fire continuously.

BOOST

SHIFT or the BOOST button gives a burst of speed and brief invulnerability. Watch the energy meter.

SPECIAL

E or the SPECIAL button unleashes your ship's unique ability once energy is full.

POWER-UPS

Destroyed enemies sometimes drop power-ups. Fly into them to collect instantly.

PAUSE

P or the pause button freezes the action any time. Adjust settings mid-run.
